- Teal (accent): `#00C2A8`
- Paper (background): `#F7F4EF`
- Ink (body): `#1A1A1A`

## Type
- Display / titles: `"Cormorant Garamond", Georgia, serif` — 28–36px, weight 600
- Body: `"DM Sans", system-ui, sans-serif` — 14–16px, weight 400
- Labels / meta: `"DM Sans"`, 11–12px, uppercase tracking 0.08em, color muted
- Price: display face, teal or navy, never muted

## Spacing
- Page padding: 48px
- Section gap: 28px
- Logo height: 40px (default). Enlarge if comments say too small.
- Footer top rule: 1px `#D4CFC6`, then 16px gap

2. Title block: client + engagement title
3. Scope bullets (3–5)
4. Investment / price (prominent)
5. Terms (short)
6. Footer: legal name · TIN · email · phone · Kampala
