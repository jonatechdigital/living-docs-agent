### [2026-11-21] kelh-brochure-inspo.png
- Source: `uploads/kelh-brochure-inspo.png`
- Multi-page brochure grid, strong section rhythm.
- 1) Opening spread: left summary/TOC with numbered list + small image grid; right About with large vertical photo + short bullets.
- 2) Services spread: two-column copy; center cluster of 4 icon tiles; opposite page hero photo over quote block.
- 3) Team: 2x3 portrait grid with name/title under each; opposite page chart/bars + two text columns.
- 4) Process: image collage left, two-column body right.
- 5) Case study: big metric/headline + small icon row; image+text wrap on facing page.
- 6) Projects gallery: numbered captions over photos; one dark full-bleed panel.
- 7) Testimonials: circular avatars beside quotes.
- 8) Pricing close: 3 vertical plan cards (middle emphasized) + final CTA/contact block.
- Density: airy, lots of white space, clear page-pair hierarchy. Above fold per page = section title + one dominant visual.

### [2026-11-21] e78014aa_kelh-brochure-inspo.png
- Source: `uploads/e78014aa_kelh-brochure-inspo.png`
- **Section Order:** Progresses linearly across spreads from Executive Summary & About Us, to Services, Team & Capabilities, Methodology/Process, In-Depth Case Study, Project Showcase, Client References/Testimonials, and concludes with Pricing Plans & Terms.
- **Hierarchy:** Primary section headings anchor the top-left margins; secondary emphasis is driven by large photographic blocks or highlighted metric callouts; tertiary information is organized into structured text columns, numbered cards, and micro-data lists.
- **Grid & Columns:** Structured on a multi-column modular grid across two-page spreads, shifting fluidly between 2-column split layouts (text vs. image), 3-column arrangements (team member cards, pricing tiers), and 4-unit data blocks (service features, metrics).
- **Density:** Medium-to-high information density, pairing compact multi-paragraph text blocks, progress meters, and tabular pricing matrices with structured whitespace gutters and image containers.
- **Upper-Third / Initial Visual Entry:** Dominated by primary spread titles, introductory summary abstracts, and large-format architectural or portrait photography before subdividing into detailed operational copy below.
